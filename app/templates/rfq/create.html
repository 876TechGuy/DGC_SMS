Two-Step Approval Workflow
Step 1: Quotation Upload

Quotations are uploaded to the RFQ. The system automatically routes the RFQ to the Specification Reviewer.

Step 2: Specification Review

The designated Specification Reviewer examines each quotation to verify compliance with requirements and specifications. They can:

  • Approve the quotation (meets specifications)
  • Reject the quotation (does not comply)
  • Request clarification or corrections
Step 3: Branch Head Approval

After all quotations are reviewed, the Branch Head provides final approval. They can:

  • Approve the RFQ for supplier selection and award
  • Reject the entire RFQ
  • Request additional clarification
Step 4: Supplier Selection

Once fully approved, the RFQ is ready for supplier selection and purchase order generation.
